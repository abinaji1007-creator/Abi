KPI Formula Guide

SEC Submitted: count of SEC Form IDs/records by Job Code.

SEC Approved by SI: Approved SEC count + (Expired SEC count − Expired SECs with blank EHSIncharge).

SEC Approved by EHS: Status = Approved.

Physical Verification: Verification Mode = Physical.

Virtual Verification: Approved by EHS − Physical Verification. Negative values are flagged.

Rejected & Expired by EHS: Expired SEC records where “Is the SEC Rejected By” identifies EHS.

SEC with Observation: distinct extractable SEC IDs from Observation Report SECFormDetails, excluding non-SEC values.

Observation in SEC Not Available: observation records whose SECFormDetails indicates SEC NOT AVAILABLE.

Expired in SI Flow: SEC Submitted − SEC Approved by SI.

SEC Mandate: SEC Submitted + SEC Not Available observations.

Ineffective Control: Rejected & Expired by EHS + SEC with Observation + SEC Not Available + Expired in SI Flow. This is intentionally additive; overlaps are not removed.

Implementation %: ((Mandate − Ineffective Control) / Mandate) × 100. If Mandate = 0, N/A.
